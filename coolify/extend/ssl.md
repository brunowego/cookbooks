## Let’s Encrypt

```sh
scp /path/to/your/domain.cert root@your-server-ip:/data/coolify/proxy/certs/domain.cert
scp /path/to/your/domain.key root@your-server-ip:/data/coolify/proxy/certs/domain.key
```
